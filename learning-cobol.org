** What's cobol ?

- COBOL (Common Business-Oriented Language)

#+begin_src cobol
IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.

    DISPLAY "Hello, business world!".

- Mainframe Platforms
  + z/OS and OS/390 : IBM's mainframe operating systems where most enterprise cobol runs
  + *JCL* (Job Control Language) mediates the relationship between COBOL programs and the OS
  + Mainframe COBOL often *interfaces* with CICS, IMS, DB2, and other *mainframe subsystems
    
#+begin_quote
COBOL program
   ├── JCL / JES  → batch job execution
   ├── CICS       → online transaction processing
   ├── DB2        → relational database access
   └── IMS        → transaction processing or hierarchical databases
#+end_quote

    MOVE 40.00 TO HOURS-WORKED.

    DISPLAY "Weekly Pay: $" WEEKLY-PAY.
    STOP RUN.
#+end_src

1. What do the numbers 9(5)V99 mean in the PICTURE clause ?
:NOTES:
That's really fun just repeat and you know what format the number has
:END:

#+begin_quote
- =9= means **one numeric digit**, from =0= through =9=.
- =(5)= repeats that digit five times: =99999=
- =V= means an **implied decimal point**. It determines where the decimal is logically placed but usually isn’t stored or displayed as a character.
- =9= provides two digits after the decimal point.
#+end_quote

2. Why is STOP RUN included at the end ?

#+begin_quote
=STOP RUN= tells COBOL to **end the program’s execution** and return control to the operating system. Similar to =return 0;= in C

- A subprogram that was called by another COBOL program usually uses:
  =cobol GOBACK=
#+end_quote
